Short answers on reaching the team and following platform changes.

## How do I get help?

Open an in-product ticket from the dashboard - the fastest path for anything involving a resource - or email support@miosa.ai for non-resource questions.
See [Support](/docs/support).

## What should I include in a request?

The resource id, the operation you ran, a timestamp with timezone, the `x-request-id`, and the exact error message.
See [Support](/docs/support).

## Where can I see status and changes?

The status page for live health, the [changelog](/docs/changelog) for shipped changes, and [Known limitations](/docs/known-limitations) for current gaps.
See [Support](/docs/support).

## Where is the community?

Join the MIOSA Discord for questions and announcements.

## Still stuck?

- [Ask OSA](/docs/support): Ask OSA is built into every docs page - use the button in the header for instant answers grounded in these docs.
- [Email support](mailto:support@miosa.ai): Open a ticket from the dashboard, or email the support address. Include the resource id, operation, timestamp, x-request-id, and exact error.
- [Join Discord](https://discord.gg/miosa): Ask in the MIOSA Discord, where the team and other builders hang out.
