Inside Optimal, the sidebar switches to Optimal's own pages.
Each page shows the current workspace.

| Page | What it keeps |
|---|---|
| **New** | Starts a fresh chat |
| **Tasks** | What Optimal took on in your chats, grouped by chat |
| **Investigations** | The investigations Optimal ran, with their evidence |
| **Artifacts** | Everything Optimal produced for you |
| **Approvals** | Approvals across your chats; see [Approvals](/docs/optimal/approvals) |
| **History** | Every chat, and what Optimal did in the workspace |
| **Usage** | Spend and tokens; see [Pricing and usage](/docs/optimal/pricing) |
| **Preferences** | Approvals, memory, chat history, notifications, and sign-ins |

## Tasks

Tasks lists what Optimal took on, grouped by the chat it came from.
The page refreshes while something is still running.
A running group of tasks can be cancelled from here.

When there are none yet, the page reads "No durable tasks yet".
Ask Optimal to investigate, plan, or operate something and its task appears.

## Investigations and Artifacts

Both pages have a search box and a status filter.

- **Review investigation** opens an investigation and the evidence behind it.
- **Open artifact** opens something Optimal produced, such as a report or a file.

When a list is empty, **Start in Optimal** opens a new chat.

## History

History lists every chat in the workspace.
Search by title, open a chat to pick it up again, or use **Load older chats** to go further back.
Each row has a menu to rename or delete the chat, and **Copy chat ID**.

Below the chats, **Workspace events** lists what Optimal did in the workspace.

## Preferences

| Section | Setting | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Approvals | Ask before | Every step, Changes only, Deletions only |
| About you | Remember across chats | On or off; your saved memories are listed with **Delete** |
| Chat history | Keep chats for | 30 days, 90 days, 1 year |
| Notifications | Needs your approval, Work finished | On or off |
| Sign-ins | Saved sign-ins | Masked site names, with **Remove** |

Memories are yours: only you see them, and only in this workspace.
Turn **Remember across chats** off to stop Optimal keeping new ones.

Chats older than the **Keep chats for** setting can no longer be resumed.
