## How it works

1. **Learn**Read, play and watch. Everything in Learn is free and open to everyone.
2. **Pass the final**Each item has a short final. Questions are different every time and graded on our side.
3. **Get credits**Passing adds credits straight to your workspace balance.

## The fine print, in plain words

- Credits land in your workspace balance and work for anything you run on MIOSA, including Optimal.
- They expire 180 days after you earn them.
- The limit is $100 per person and $100 per organization, for life.
- You need a verified email and an account at least a day old. If you pass before that, the reward waits and arrives on its own.
- Finals draw new questions each time and are graded by us. After a miss you wait a little before trying again.
- The person must complete it themselves. No AI agents, scripts or other people.
- Videos count when you watch them at normal speed in a visible tab. Skipping ahead does not count.
- Credits cannot be withdrawn or refunded to a card.
